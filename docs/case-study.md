# From marketing execution to measurable growth learning

This project is associated with a 41-page case study of **Enrich Labs**, an AI marketing software product serving Shopify-oriented sellers. The study investigates its public-facing positioning, competitive context, product-design opportunities, and the measurement gap between executing marketing activity and understanding incremental effects.

## Strategic contribution

- Establish a Shopify-first entry point that minimizes marketing execution friction.
- Record marketing interventions with clearly defined treatment, comparison, and measurement periods.
- Offer transparent estimates, caveat detection, decision rationale, and reviewable next-action recommendations.
- Progress carefully from a simple CSV-based proof of concept to proper experiment design, connectors, evaluation, and governance.

## What was implemented

The **Streamlit prototype** implements CSV ingestion, deterministic pandas calculations, relative growth comparisons, explainability cards, and simple sensitivity analysis. It does not implement the long-term roadmap modules, external integrations, autonomous execution, a causal forest, or formal causal identification checks.
